Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Matt Crowder's Training App ("the app") is a personal app for viewing a training program and logging workouts in the user's own Google Sheet.

What the app accesses

The app uses Google Sign-In and requests the Google Sheets scope. It uses that access only to read and write the user's own training spreadsheet. It does not access any other Google data.

What the app collects

The app has no backend server. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data or Google user data with anyone. Spreadsheet data goes directly between the device and Google's servers.

Sign-in tokens

Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the device: in the iOS Keychain on iOS, and in browser storage on the web. They are never sent anywhere other than Google.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access to your Google account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
